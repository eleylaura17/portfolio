Spotify

Listening Parties

Unlocking deeper connection and commerce by transforming how fans and artists engage with music.

The Challenge

People come to Spotify to listen, but what if listening could unlock deeper moments of connection, and connection could unlock commerce?

My Role

Lead Content Designer — I owned all content and decisions on how it was displayed across the product experience.

What I Did

  • Created a comprehensive content map covering JTBD, stakeholder questions, edge cases, and tone theory
  • Annotated Figma wireframes with targeted copy prompts, enabling faster iteration and clearer testing paths
  • Drafted positioning and collaborated with Product, Legal, Marketing, and Research to align on brand goals
  • Wrote and tested invite, transactional, and retargeting emails
  • Led landing page redesign and refined CTAs to improve conversion at the final moment

Impact

  • 60% active engagement
  • Landing page redesign: 32% → 65% willingness to continue
  • 43% RSVP rate after CTA refinements (up from 22%)
  • 90% fan satisfaction, 85% artist satisfaction in UX testing
  • 125% lift in weekly streaming, 444% spike in saves on event day
Spotify

Merch on Spotify

Building a scalable commerce platform that unlocks a new revenue stream for artists and introduces fans to shopping on Spotify.

The Challenge

Fans have a relationship with Spotify through listening, but building a commerce relationship is an entirely different story. There was already poor user sentiment toward the existing merch solution.

My Role

Lead Content Designer — I owned all content and decisions on how it was displayed across the product experience.

What I Did

  • Led competitive, internal, and behavioral audits analyzing browsing flows, purchase patterns, and copy consistency
  • Partnered with design in early stages to shape direction and lay groundwork for shopping attribution
  • Conducted deep analysis of fan marketing campaigns with Data Science to assess engagement vs. effort
  • Built a data-backed foundation for product content using high-performing words, phrases, and CTAs
  • Strategically developed shop.spotify domain naming to be short, intuitive, and future-ready
  • Crafted a web-based "superstore" to enable fan browsing without engineering lifts

Impact

  • 12K+ artists enrolled within the first 6 months
  • $100K in merch revenue generated in the first 2 months
  • 3,792% increase in merch sales
  • Onboarding time reduced from 2 hours to 5 minutes
  • Strong press coverage and overwhelmingly positive fan and artist feedback

WMG

Content Generation

Making motion art scalable beyond the top 100 artists through intelligent workflow design.

The Challenge

Motion art is a relatively new asset type that Spotify and Apple Music are investing in. Catalog releases with these assets added would lift streams by 1-2%. This number may be small, but it's on a very large base of digital streaming. The current manual process is not scalable for artists past the top 100.

My Role

Lead UX Designer — I owned all UX decisions from discovery to implementation.

What I Did

  • Ran user interview sessions on their current workflow
  • Designed 3 UX pathways with considerations around scope and engineering constraints
  • Led feedback sessions with users, product managers, and fellow designers
  • Owned storytelling to leadership team

Impact

  • ~1% uplift across 300 catalog albums

Internal LLM

The Challenge

Most employees at WMG still struggle to access the information they need to do their jobs well. Data analysts have limited bandwidth to support the never-ending stream of requests.

My Role

Lead UX Designer — I owned all UX decisions from discovery to implementation.
